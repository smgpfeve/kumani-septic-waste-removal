Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and processes.

How often should I clean my septic tank?

Most residential septic tanks should be cleaned every 3–5 years, depending on household size and usage. Larger families or high-usage properties may need more frequent service.

What signs indicate my septic tank needs cleaning?

Slow drains, unpleasant odors around the tank area, pooling water near the drain field, and gurgling sounds in plumbing are all warning signs that your tank may need attention.

Do you offer emergency services?

Yes! We provide 24/7 emergency response for septic backups, overflows, and urgent waste removal. Call us anytime and we'll dispatch a team as quickly as possible.

How much does septic cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on tank size, location, and the level of service needed. We offer free, transparent estimates — just reach out with your details and we'll provide a quote within hours.

Do you serve commercial properties?

Absolutely. We serve restaurants, hotels, schools, hospitals, and other commercial establishments with tailored waste removal plans.

Is your waste disposal environmentally friendly?

Yes. We follow all local environmental regulations and use eco-friendly disposal methods that protect our waterways and communities.

Do you serve areas outside Nairobi?

Yes, we serve multiple regions across Kenya including Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, and surrounding areas. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific location.

What should I do before your team arrives?

Ensure the area around your septic tank is accessible and clear of obstacles. If possible, mark the tank location so our crew can find it quickly.
